    ## What data we collect


    **As a guest visiting the site:**

    - Anonymous subscription statistics (Cloudflare Analytics, cookie-free)

    - Hashed IP address when you click "Reveal Contact" (anti-spam only, doesn't
    identify you personally)


    **If you subscribe to the newsletter:**

    - Email address (we keep it only while you're subscribed)


    **If you submit a query through a form (e.g., tour booking):**

    - Name, email, phone, date, number of people, message


    **As a property owner with an account:**

    - Email, phone, property details, photos, prices


    ## How we use your data


    - **Statistics** — to understand which pages interest visitors and improve
    the platform

    - **Newsletter** — we send updates and recommendations occasionally (max
    once a month)

    - **Tour booking queries** — we forward them directly to the tour operator
    and keep them locally for our records

    - **Owner data** — we use it for billing and communication


    ## Who we share data with


    **We do not sell** data to third parties. We share only with:

    - Hosting (Cloudflare) — to keep the site running

    - Email service — for sending newsletters

    - Property owners — when you submit an inquiry form (to them only)


    ## Your rights


    You can request:

    - To see what data we have about you

    - To delete it

    - To unsubscribe from the newsletter (link in every email)


    Write to: [privacy@visitpetrovac.com](mailto:privacy@visitpetrovac.com)


    ## Cookies


    The site uses **only technically necessary cookies** (session, language). We
    don't use tracking pixels, we don't have Google Analytics, we don't have
    Facebook Pixel.

## Koje podatke prikupljamo

**Kao gost koji posjećuje sajt:**
- Anonimne statistike pretplate (Cloudflare Analytics, bez kolačića)
- Hashovana IP adresa kad klikneš "Otkrij kontakt" (samo za anti-spam, ne identifikuje te lično)

**Ako se prijaviš na newsletter:**
- Email adresa (čuvamo samo dok si pretplaćen)

**Ako pošalješ upit kroz formu (npr. tour booking):**
- Ime, email, telefon, datum, broj osoba, poruka

**Kao vlasnik objekta sa nalogom:**
- Email, telefon, podaci o objektu, fotografije, cijene

## Kako koristimo podatke

- **Statistike** — da znamo koje stranice ljude zanimaju i da poboljšamo platformu
- **Newsletter** — šaljemo povremeno (max jednom mjesečno) novosti i preporuke
- **Tour booking upiti** — prosljeđujemo direktno vlasniku izleta i čuvamo lokalno za našu evidenciju
- **Vlasnički podaci** — koristimo za fakturisanje i komunikaciju

## Kome prosljeđujemo podatke

**Ne prodajemo** podatke trećim stranama. Prosljeđujemo samo:
- Hosting (Cloudflare) — radi rada sajta
- Email servis — za slanje newslettera
- Vlasnicima objekata — kad popuniš formu za upit (samo njima)

## Tvoja prava

Možeš tražiti:
- Da ti pokažemo koje podatke imamo o tebi
- Da ih izbrišemo
- Da odustaneš od newsletter-a (link u svakom mejlu)

Pišite na: [privacy@visitpetrovac.com](mailto:privacy@visitpetrovac.com)

## Cookies

Sajt koristi **samo tehnički neophodne kolačiće** (sesija, jezik). Ne koristimo tracking pixel-e, nemamo Google Analytics, nemamo Facebook Pixel.

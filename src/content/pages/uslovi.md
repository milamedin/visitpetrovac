    VisitPetrovac.com is an informational platform — a directory of apartments,
    restaurants, excursions and car rental services in Petrovac on the sea. We
    **do not mediate bookings** and **do not participate** in agreements between
    guests and property owners.


    ## 2. Listing content


    Each listing's content (description, photos, prices, availability) is
    maintained by the property owner themselves. VisitPetrovac does not
    guarantee information accuracy, but makes effort to regularly check content.
    If you notice inaccuracies, let us know.


    ## 3. Bookings


    All bookings are made **directly** between guest and owner. VisitPetrovac:

    - Is not a party to the contract

    - Does not take commission

    - Is not responsible for service delivery

    - Does not mediate disputes


    If you have a dispute with an owner, you can notify us and we will take
    action against the owner (including removing their listing if necessary),
    but we cannot intervene in the agreement.


    ## 4. Contact disclosure


    By clicking "Reveal contact," you agree that your IP address will be
    anonymously recorded (hashed) for statistical purposes. We do not share your
    data with third parties.


    ## 5. Prices and owner subscriptions


    All listing prices are informational. Owners may change prices — you agree
    on the final price directly with the owner.


    Owners who wish to be on the platform pay an annual subscription according
    to the price list on the [Become a partner](/postani-partner) page.


    ## 6. Intellectual property


    All content (texts, photos, design) is owned by VisitPetrovac or published
    with the owner's permission. Downloading content without permission is not
    allowed.


    ## 7. Changes to terms


    We reserve the right to change these terms. For major changes, we notify all
    registered owners by email.

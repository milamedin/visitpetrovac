    ## Petrovac Central Beach


    The most famous, the busiest, but the most practical — restaurants, bars,
    sun lounger rentals, everything within a 50m radius. Pebbly, crystal-clear
    water. **Best for:** families with small children, your first day in
    Petrovac.


    ## Lučice Beach


    5 minutes by boat from central beach (water taxi €2). Quieter, fewer crowds,
    but not many facilities — bring water and food. **Best for:** couples,
    snorkeling.


    ## Buljarica


    The longest beach in the area, around 2.5km. Sandy (a rarity on the
    Montenegrin coast!). Auto-camp and 2-3 restaurants. **Best for:** walking,
    beach sports, autumn surfing.


    ## Hidden beaches


    **Drobni Pijesak** — 10 minutes' walk south from Buljarica. Small, rocky,
    almost empty even outside peak season.


    **Dražin Vrt** — between Petrovac and Reževići, reached via a path lined
    with fishing nets. Crystal-clear sea. **Heads up:** access isn't easy, good
    shoes and 15 minutes of descent required.


    ## Practical tip


    Each beach has its own rhythm. Central is most crowded from 11am to 5pm.
    Lučice is quietest in the morning. Buljarica is most beautiful at sunset.

## Centralna plaža Petrovac

Najpoznatija, najgušća, ali najpraktičnija — restorani, bare, najam ležaljki, sve u radijusu od 50m. Šljunkovita, voda kristalno čista. **Idealna za:** porodice sa malom djecom, prvi dan u Petrovcu.

## Plaža Lučice

5 minuta brodom iz centralne plaže (taksi-brod 2€). Mirnija, manje gužve, ali nema mnogo objekata — ponesi vodu i hranu. **Idealna za:** parove, snorkling.

## Buljarica

Najduža plaža u okolini, oko 2.5km. Pješčana (rijetkost na Crnogorskoj obali!). Auto-kamp i 2-3 restorana. **Idealna za:** šetnju, sport na pijesku, surf u jesen.

## Skrivene plaže

**Drobni Pijesak** — 10 min šetnje od Buljarice ka jugu. Mala, kamenita, gotovo prazna i van vrhunca sezone.

**Dražin Vrt** — između Petrovca i Reževića, sa staze sa ribarskim mrežama. Kristalno more. **Pažnja:** prilaz nije lak, treba dobre cipele i 15 min spuštanja.

## Praktičan savjet

Svaka plaža ima drugačiji "ritam". Centralna je najgušća od 11h do 17h. Lučice najmirnije ujutru. Buljarica je najlepša pred zalazak sunca.

    ## Quick overview


    Petrovac gets quite crowded during peak season (July–August), especially on
    weekends. Parking is the biggest headache for most first-time visitors.
    Here's your complete guide.


    ## Paid parking


    **Central parking (at the old town entrance)** — €1/h, €10/day. Closest to
    the promenade and beach. Fills up quickly in season.


    **Parking behind the post office** — €0.80/h, €8/day. Smaller, quieter, 5
    minutes' walk to the beach.


    **Buljarica parking** — €0.50/h, €5/day. Cheapest option, but 15 minutes'
    walk to the main beach.


    ## Free options


    If you're visiting outside peak season (May/June, September/October), you'll
    find spots along **Obala 13. jula** street. During season — forget about it.


    ## Local tip


    Tourists' biggest mistake is arriving in Petrovac between **10am and 1pm**
    on Saturdays — that's the worst time. If you can, come before 9am or after
    7pm.

## Kratak pregled

Petrovac u sezoni (jul–avgust) postaje prilično gust, posebno vikendom. Parking je najveća muka većini gostiju koji prvi put dolaze. Evo kompletnog vodiča.

## Plaćeni parkinzi

**Centralni parking (kod ulaza u stari grad)** — 1€/h, 10€/dan. Najbliži šetalištu i plaži. U sezoni se brzo puni.

**Parking iza pošte** — 0.80€/h, 8€/dan. Manji, mirniji, 5 minuta hoda do plaže.

**Parking Buljarica** — 0.50€/h, 5€/dan. Najjeftiniji, ali 15 min hoda do centralne plaže.

## Besplatne opcije

Ako dolaziš van sezone (maj/jun, septembar/oktobar), naći ćeš mjesto duž ulice **Obala 13. jula**. U sezoni — zaboravi.

## Savjet od lokalca

Najveća greška turista je dolaziti u Petrovac između **10h i 13h** subotom — tad je najgore. Ako možeš, dođi prije 9h ili poslije 19h.
